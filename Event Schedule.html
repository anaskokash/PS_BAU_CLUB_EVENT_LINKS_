    Omar Bsaiso is a Software Engineering student at Al-Balqa Applied University, Co-Founder and Team Leader of the Problem Solving BAU Club, passionate about competitive programming and problem-solving.

    Dr. Amin Muwafi

    A graduate of Al-Balqa Applied University, Amin Muwafi is a technology leader and entrepreneur with over 20 years of experience in IT, project management, and digital transformation. He is the CTO and Co-Founder of Quality Business Solutions (QBS).

  6. 12:05 – 12:30

    Dr. Karam Ghneim · Mahmoud Zaabout · Amr Alali

  7. 12:30 – 12:40

    Guest Speaker Session

    Advisor Ruba Awni Al-Rifai

    Ruba Awni Al-Rifai is a psychological and educational consultant and social specialist, focusing on mental health, family counseling, and social development. She has participated in various conferences and community initiatives, delivering awareness sessions on women’s mental health, family issues, and child development.

  8. 12:40 – 12:55

    Honoring & Recognition Ceremony

  9. 1:00 – 2:00

    Problem Solving Challenges & Speed

    An engaging and competitive session where students take part in interactive speed challenges and problem-solving games, testing their logical thinking, quick decision-making, and teamwork. Exciting prizes and vouchers for Problem Solving training opportunities will be awarded to the winners.
